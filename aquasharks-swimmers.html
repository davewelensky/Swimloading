Getting started
1
Get the join link from your coach
Ask Britt for the Aqua Sharks join link — she'll share it via WhatsApp. Tap it on your phone and it takes you straight to sign-up. Takes about 60 seconds.
Already have an account? Tap the link while signed in and you'll be added to Aqua Sharks automatically — no new account needed.
2
Create a free SwimLoading account
Sign up with your email — no app store download needed. SwimLoading runs in your phone's browser. Once you're in, tap Add to Home Screen (in your browser menu) so it works like an app.
swimloading.com/app
3
Tap the Club tab
Once you've joined via Britt's link, a Club tab appears in the app. That's where everything lives — your results, QT progress, announcements, and upcoming galas.
What you'll see
Inside the Club tab
QT progress bars
Every event you've swum shows a bar from your first time to the SANJ standard. Your current best sits as a dot on the bar — L2, L3, and SANJ markers show exactly where you need to get to.

Time history charts
Every result across every gala plotted on a chart. Up means faster. Green dots are personal bests — the L3 line shows what you're chasing. New results appear within minutes of your coach entering them.
Personal bests
Every time you go faster the result is automatically flagged as a PB — highlighted green on your chart, shown across all your events.
Coach announcements
Britt posts training reminders, gala entry deadlines, session changes, and equipment notes here. Pinned posts stay at the top so you don't miss them.
Gala calendar
Upcoming galas with dates and descriptions. League galas are tagged separately so they're easy to spot.
Log a time trial
Train hard between galas? Log your own time trial from the app — it adds a new point to your chart and automatically checks if it's a PB.
For parents
P
Parents get their own account
You don't need to share your swimmer's login. Ask Britt for the join link, create your own SwimLoading account, and once your account is linked to your swimmer's roster entry you'll see all their results and QT progress directly — without needing to borrow their phone.
Parent linking is coming. For now, ask Britt to link your account to your swimmer's roster entry in the admin. This takes her about 30 seconds.
Adding to your home screen
A
iPhone (Safari)
Open swimloading.com/app in Safari. Tap the Share button (the box with an arrow pointing up), then scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen. SwimLoading appears on your home screen like a normal app — no App Store needed.
B
Android (Chrome)
Open swimloading.com/app in Chrome. Tap the three dots menu in the top right, then tap Add to Home Screen. Same result — works like a native app.
