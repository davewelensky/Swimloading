Getting started
1
Get the join link from your coach
Ask Britt for the Aqua Sharks join link — she'll share it via WhatsApp. Tap it on your phone and it takes you straight to sign-up. Takes about 60 seconds.
Already have an account? Tap the link while signed in and you'll be added to Aqua Sharks automatically — no new account needed.
2
Create a free SwimLoading account
Sign up with your email — no app store download needed. SwimLoading runs in your phone's browser. Once you're in, tap Add to Home Screen (in your browser menu) so it works like an app.
swimloading.com/app
3
Tap the Club tab
Once you've joined via Britt's link, a Club tab appears in the app. That's where everything lives — your results, QT progress, announcements, and upcoming galas.
What you'll see
Inside the Club tab
QT progress bars
Every event you've swum shows a bar from your first time to the SANJ standard. Your current best sits as a dot on the bar — L2, L3, and SANJ markers show exactly where you need to get to.

Time history charts
Every result across every gala plotted on a chart. Up means faster. Green dots are personal bests — the L3 line shows what you're chasing. New results appear within minutes of your coach entering them.
Personal bests
Every time you go faster the result is automatically flagged as a PB — highlighted green on your chart, shown across all your events.
Coach announcements
Britt posts training reminders, gala entry deadlines, session changes, and equipment notes here. Pinned posts stay at the top so you don't miss them.
Gala calendar
Upcoming galas with dates and descriptions. League galas are tagged separately so they're easy to spot.
Log a time trial
Train hard between galas? Log your own time trial from the app — it adds a new point to your chart and automatically checks if it's a PB.
For parents
P1
Get the parent join link from Britt
Ask Britt for the parent join link — it's different from the swimmer link. She can WhatsApp or email it to you. It looks like:
swimloading.com/join/aqua-sharks-atlantic?role=parent
P2
Create your own SwimLoading account
Tap the link and sign in or create a free account using your own email. You don't share a login with your swimmer — each person has their own account.
P3
Find your swimmer and request access
After signing in you'll be asked to find your swimmer. Type their name, select them from the list, and choose your relationship — Mother, Father, Guardian, or whatever fits. Then tap Send access request.
If you have more than one swimmer at Aqua Sharks, tap Link another swimmer after the first request and repeat for each child.
P4
Britt approves — then you're in
Your request goes to Britt's admin panel. Once she approves it, open SwimLoading and tap the Club tab. You'll see your swimmer's full profile — QT progress, gala results, time charts, upcoming events, and squad announcements. Read-only, always up to date.
The Club tab shows a purple "Following: [swimmer name]" card when your access is approved. If it still shows pending, Britt hasn't approved yet — give her a nudge.
Big news New at Aqua Sharks
SwimBetter Analysis Sessions
Powered by EOLabs

Aqua Sharks is partnering with EOLabs to offer SwimBETTER analysis sessions. A small handset is worn during your Saturday pool session — it measures exactly how your body moves through the water, what's slowing you down, and where your power is going.

Efficiency %
Propulsion efficiency — how much of your energy actually moves you forward
Watts (PPS)
Stroke power output — peak propulsive power per stroke
Force map
Where your force goes — downward push, hand drag, lateral waste
Stroke path
Symmetry, timing, and distance per stroke across each length
How it works
1 Book your session — Saturday pool sessions at Vetch's
2 Swim your normal session — the handset captures every stroke
3 Receive your full technical report — efficiency, power, force distribution, stroke path
4 6-week follow-up session — swim again and see what's improved
