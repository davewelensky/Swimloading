Getting started
1
Log into your admin panel
Go to swimloading.com/club-admin/duc and sign in with your SwimLoading account (the same one you use to log temperatures). You'll land straight on the DUC dashboard.
swimloading.com/club-admin/duc
2
Get your members on the platform
Go to the Join Link tab. Copy the link and send it to your members via WhatsApp or email. When they tap it and log in with their SwimLoading account, they're automatically added to DUC — race number assigned from the existing membership list. No manual admin needed.
What the join link does
Links their SwimLoading account to DUC Matches by existing membership roster
Assigns their race number automatically From the imported member list
Unlocks their Club tab in the app They see standings, events, and their category
How members log a swim
3
Open the app and tap Log
Members go to swimloading.com/app (or open it from their home screen). On the dashboard there's a Log Conditions button — tap it, pick the spot, enter the water temperature, and submit. That's it. Each log earns points toward the Temp Challenge automatically.
What they enter
Spot Any open water spot — beach, dam, pool
Water temperature In °C — just a quick reading
Conditions (optional) Swell, wind, visibility
swimloading.com/app
4
Add the app to your home screen
Important: you must open swimloading.com/app first — then add it to your home screen. If you add from the main website, the shortcut will open the website, not the app.
iPhone (Safari)
Step 1 Open Safari and go to swimloading.com/app
Step 2 Tap the Share button (box with arrow) at the bottom
Step 3 Tap Add to Home Screen — then tap Add
Android (Chrome)
Step 1 Open Chrome and go to swimloading.com/app
Step 2 Tap the 3-dot menu (top right)
Step 3 Tap Add to Home Screen — then tap Add
swimloading.com/app — open this first
Running the temp challenge
5
The challenge runs itself
Go to the Temp Challenge tab. It automatically tracks every DUC member who logs water temperatures — no setup required. Use the month picker to view any of the last 4 months. The leaderboard updates in real time as members log.
How points work
Every log Any swim, any spot, any time
10 pts
Unique spot bonus Per different spot logged per day
+10 pts
Breakdown by category
The leaderboard shows both an overall ranking and a separate standings table for each category.
Guppies Sailfish Makos Walrus Coelacanths
6
Pick your winner at month end
On the last day of the month, open the Temp Challenge tab and check who's at the top. The platform shows you exactly who logged the most and how many points they earned. Announce the winner and award the prize — that part's on you. Nothing to configure or close off in the system.
League races
7
Enter race results after each race
Go to the League tab. Click Enter results to record finishing positions for a race. Season standings update automatically across all categories. Members can see their own standing and points in the app.
8
Members pre-enter races from the app
Once members have joined via the link, they'll see upcoming league races in their Club tab with an Enter button. Pre-entries show up in your admin under the relevant event — useful for planning.
What your members see
Club tab in the SwimLoading app
  • Their DUC membership card — club name, city, race number, category badge
  • Personal season standings — position, points, qualification status
  • Upcoming league races with a one-tap Enter button
  • Link to view the full DUC league standings
Members tab
9
Manage the team from one place
The Members tab shows everyone who's joined via the link. From here you can reassign categories, update race numbers, and change roles (member / organiser / admin). If someone's in the wrong category, you can fix it here — no impact on historical results.
