The UK has hundreds of open water clubs and thousands of committed swimmers. What it doesn't have is structured water temperature data — logged by the people in the water, at the spots they actually swim.

Your WhatsApp group tracks the water. SwimLoading makes it permanent.
Every temperature your members log becomes part of a real, dated, searchable club record — at your spots, not a buoy miles offshore.
What your club gets
The platform. Not the promises.
Water Temperature Log
Members log the real water temp at your club's spots — lakes, rivers, sea. Every entry is dated, mapped, and visible to the whole squad. Build the record no one else has.
Live Conditions
Wind direction and speed, wave height, water temperature — at your venues, updated hourly. One link to share before every session.
Group Sessions
Post your weekly swims. Members see spot, time, distance, and that day's conditions. RSVP so you know who's coming in the water.
Club Leaderboard
Who's swimming most, who's logging the most temperatures. Friendly competition that keeps your squad engaged and your data growing.
Session Log
Every swim tracked — spot, distance, time, conditions on the day. A permanent record for every member, automatically tied to their temperature logs.
Strava Import
Members who use Strava import their swim and add what Strava doesn't capture — the water temperature and conditions. Two tools, one record.
How it works
Simple to run, valuable over time.
1
Your club joins SwimLoading
We add your club's venues to the map. Members sign up free — no per-seat fees, no limits on who can join.
2
Members log what they find
After each swim: a tap to log the water temp, conditions, and distance. Takes 20 seconds. Every entry builds your club's dataset.
3
The data compounds
After a season your club has something no other club has — a real, community-sourced water temperature record at your spots. Year two, it's genuinely valuable for acclimatisation planning, safety, and squad management.
