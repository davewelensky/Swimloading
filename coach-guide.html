Your coaching
guide

How to mark attendance, cover sessions, and add walk-ins — right from the pool deck.

Open Coach Attendance
Before your first session
1
Ask Britt to add you as a coach
She adds your email in the Team tab — that gives you access. You don't need a SwimLoading account first, it gets created automatically when you sign in.
2
Open the coach link and sign in
Open swimloading.com/coach/aqua-sharks-atlantic in Safari. Enter your email address and tap Send sign-in link.
How sign-in works
You get an email with a link Check your inbox — it arrives within a minute
Tap the link in the email It opens the coach page in Safari with you signed in — no password needed
Already signed in? Next time you open the page your session is remembered — no need to sign in again
Important: tap the email link while on your phone — it opens the coach page, not your email app. Don't copy-paste the link into a browser manually.
3
Add to your home screen — from the coach page
Once you're signed in and can see your sessions, tap Share → Add to Home Screen. You'll get a Coach icon that opens the attendance page in one tap.
Add to home screen after signing in and from the coach page — not from the sign-in screen and not from the main SwimLoading app.
Marking attendance
4
Open the coach page during warm-up
The home screen shows today's sessions. Your sessions appear under My sessions — matched by the name Britt assigned to you in the timetable. Each tile shows the expected swimmer count and a trial count badge if you have trial swimmers in that session.
My Squad at the bottom of the home screen lists any trial swimmers by name and flags overdue fees — so you know what to look out for before you even tap Start.
5
Tap your session and hit Start
Check the squad name and time on the confirm screen, edit your name if it's wrong, then tap Start to open the register.
6
Mark each swimmer with one tap
Four status buttons — tap the right one for each name.
Status codes
HERE Swimmer showed up and swam
NC No contact — absent with no notice given
EXCUSE Let you know in advance they'd miss the session
AWAY Pre-arranged absence — holiday, travel, injury
7
Save when done
Tap Save — Britt's admin updates immediately and the done screen shows a summary of who was marked.
Walk-ins and trials
8
Tap Walk-in in the bottom bar
A search panel opens — type the swimmer's name to look them up.
9
Add a roster swimmer from another squad
If they're already on the roster (just a different squad), tap Add next to their name — they appear in your register for this session.
10
Add a brand-new trial swimmer
If they're not on the roster at all, type their full name and tap Add as new trial swimmer — they're added to the register immediately with a TRIAL badge.
Walk-in trial swimmers appear in Britt's roster after you save. She'll assign a squad and set their trial dates.
11
Made a mistake? Remove them
Walk-in swimmers show a × button next to their name — tap it to remove them before you save.
Covering a session
12
Cover sessions show on your home screen
Sessions not assigned to you appear under Cover a session — tap any one to take it on.
13
Enter your name on the confirm screen
The confirm screen shows whose session it normally is — update the coach name to yours, then tap Start.
14
Mark and save as normal
The register saves under your name for this session — Britt can see who covered it in her attendance view.
