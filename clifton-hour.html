The Format

Your hour. Your swim.

A supported open-water swim from Clifton 4th, every Wednesday evening. No compulsory distance, no race pressure. Get in, swim your session, get out when you're done.

Safety

Safety comes first.

Every swim is run by K8 Coaching's water-safety team.

Swimmer check-in and check-out, every session.
Quick briefing for anyone swimming with us for the first time.
Water-safety team in and on the water throughout.
