What is SwimLoading?

Water temperature, not guesswork

Every venue carries a temperature where we can get one — measured by swimmers, or modelled from the sea itself. That is the number that decides whether a swim is enjoyable or dangerous, and almost nobody publishes it.

We show our working

Each listing says how well it was verified and when it was last checked. A swim read off an organiser's own page is labelled differently from one we merely found. You can see the difference before you book flights.

Log your own swims

The app tracks the water you actually swim in — temperature, distance, conditions — and every reading you log improves the picture for the swimmer after you.
