The Audience

Not casual swimmers. Committed ones.

Every SwimLoading member is self-selected. They downloaded a niche PWA, set up an account, and come back regularly to log conditions. This is not passive scrolling.

Data-Driven Swimmers
Log temps, track hazards, check conditions before every swim. They make purchasing decisions based on information — including what gear to buy.
Event Seekers
145 upcoming swims planned through the app. These people show up — they coordinate, train, and travel for the right swim experience.
Spread Across Africa
Cape Town, Durban, PE, Kenton, Garden Route, Namibia. 10 regions and growing — not a Cape Town bubble.
Performance Focused
Tracking streaks, competing on leaderboards. Athletes who invest in gear, nutrition, training, and experiences.
