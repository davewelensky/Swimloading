How points work
More swims, more spots, more points
10 pts
Per log
Every temperature you log at any spot earns 10 points — pool, ocean, dam, river, anywhere.
+10 pts
New spot, same day
Log at a second (or third) different spot on the same day and earn an extra 10 per unique spot.
Example: Log at Vetch's Beach (10 pts) and then at Blue Lagoon the same day (+10 pts) = 30 points for that day. Standings reset each month.

Common questions
FAQ
Do I need a second app?
No. SwimLoading is one app — swimloading.com/app. Club membership is a layer on top of the same account. You log exactly as you always have. The DUC challenge leaderboard at swimloading.com/clubs/duc is a web page you can bookmark or add to your home screen.
I'm already on SwimLoading. Do my past logs count?
The challenge is monthly — it counts logs from the current calendar month. Once you've joined DUC, every log you make from that point counts. Logs before you joined don't count retroactively, but you're up and running from the moment you confirm.
What category will I be in?
Categories are assigned by age at the time you join: Guppies (under 18), Sailfish (18–29), Makos (30–39), Walrus (40–49), Coelacanths (50+). Make sure your date of birth is set in your SwimLoading profile. If it's not, contact Steve — he can reassign you from the admin panel.
Does the pool count?
Yes. Log at any spot on SwimLoading — ocean, pool, dam, river. Points are the same regardless of where you swim. Log what you actually swim in.
How do I enter a race?
Upcoming DUC events appear in the app. Tap the event and pre-enter — your permanent race number is confirmed instantly and Steve has your name on the start list. Day-of entries are still available at the registration table.
