Common questions
Do members pay separately?
No. R799/month covers the whole club — all members get full SwimLoading app access included. No per-head billing. Annual billing saves you R2,400.
How long does setup take?
About 5 minutes. We configure your club page, link your home water, and give you a member invite link. After that, it's entirely self-managed.
What about race day — do you track who's in the water?
No, and we won't claim to. SwimLoading owns the admin before the gun — pre-entry, race numbers, start list. Who's in the water and who's out safe belongs to your race officials and NSRI (087 094 9774). We don't try to own that.
What if we want to cancel?
Cancel anytime, no lock-in. Your club page and member data remain accessible for 30 days — enough time to export anything you need.
