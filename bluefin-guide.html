Your coach
admin guide

Roster management, daily attendance, your weekly timetable, training sets, and announcements: everything you need to run Bluefin without a spreadsheet.

Open Bluefin Admin
Every day
1
Check the Overview: your weekly command centre
When you open the admin panel, the Overview tab is your starting point. The top strip shows this week's sessions across every day. Click any day bar to switch the session list below it.
What Overview shows
Action items Fees overdue, trials expiring this week, swimmers not seen in 3 weeks: each alert links directly to the affected members
Week at a glance Mon–Sat bar chart: fill % per day. Click any day to see that day's sessions below
Session detail Tap any session row to see who's assigned: TRIAL and FEE DUE badges per swimmer, then mark attendance from the same panel
Squad health Last 30 days attendance rate with trend (up or down vs the 30 days before)
KPI strip: always visible at the top
Today's fill Enrolled vs total capacity across all today's sessions
Attendance rate Last 30 days across the whole club
2
Mark attendance after each session
Go to the Attendance tab, select the session, and mark each swimmer. Status codes match your existing register.
Status codes
Present: attended the session
NC (No Contact): absent, no notice given
Notice: gave advance notice of absence
No Show: expected, did not arrive
Away: scheduled absence (holiday, travel)
DNP (Did Not Participate): present but did not swim
Catch-up: attended a different session than usual (e.g. open water instead of pool)
Setting up
3
Import your roster from a spreadsheet
In the Roster tab, click Import CSV. Copy your member list, paste it in, map the columns, and import. Existing members are updated, new ones are added. Run it multiple times safely; it won't create duplicates.
What the importer handles
Safe to re-run Re-import any time to fix details: existing records update, nothing duplicates
Flexible columns Map Name, Member No, DOB, Gender, Phone, Trial, Fee Paid
4
Your Masters Squad is already set up
One squad is loaded for Bluefin, Masters Squad, matching how you actually train: one shared base set that faster and slower swimmers scale themselves, rather than separate named squads. Go to Settings → Squads if you ever want to set a capacity limit or add a second squad (for example, if the kids' programme needs its own).
5
Your weekly timetable is already loaded
Settings → Weekly Timetable already has your real sessions: Pool Squad Mon/Wed/Fri 7:45–8:45am, and Tuesday Open Water 7:00–8:00am. Add or edit sessions there any time: pick the day, start and end time, session type, and coach. These are the sessions Attendance is marked against.
Managing members
6
Trial swimmers and fee tracking
In the Roster tab, each row has inline toggles you can tap without opening the edit modal. The Overview alerts you when a trial is expiring within 7 days or a fee is overdue. Clicking the alert takes you straight to the filtered list.
Inline toggles on every roster row
TRIAL × Tap to remove trial status immediately: matches your R150 drop-in sessions before someone commits to the monthly R700
UNPAID × Tap to mark fee as paid. Shows as + unpaid when fee is paid; tap to flag as owing.
7
Get swimmers onto the platform
Go to the Join Link tab and copy the swimmer link. Send it via WhatsApp. When they sign up and tap the link, they're matched to their roster entry automatically. No manual linking needed.
What joining unlocks for a swimmer
Links to the roster Their session history appears immediately
Their training schedule Pool, open water and any assigned sets, in the app
Your announcements Session changes, equipment reminders: pinned to the top of their Club tab
8
Track who has actually joined
The Members tab shows every swimmer who has created a SwimLoading account and linked to Bluefin, a live subset of your Roster. Think of Roster as your paper register and Members as the digital view.
Tip: If someone's history isn't showing up, check they appear in Members. It means the join link worked. If they're on Roster but not Members, they haven't completed sign-up yet.
9
Monitor attendance and training: Squad Tracker
Squad Tracker gives you a bird's-eye view of Masters Squad: sessions attended and sets completed over time. Click any swimmer row to open their individual profile.
Keeping the squad informed
10
Post announcements
Go to Announcements. Write a title and message: session changes, equipment lists, Langebaan or Robben Island prep reminders. Pin important notices to keep them at the top. Appears immediately in every swimmer's Club tab.
Good examples
Session change No Tuesday open water this week: sea conditions
Race prep Langebaan Express entries close 1 October, get yours in
Managing coaches
11
Give another coach access to mark attendance
Coaches don't need a SwimLoading account first, it's created automatically when they sign in. Add them in the Team tab → Coaching Staff → Add coach → enter their email, then send them the coach link: swimloading.com/coach/bluefin.
How a coach signs in (first time)
They open the link and enter their email The page sends a sign-in link by email: no password needed
Add to home screen from there Share → Add to Home Screen while on the coach page
Tip: The coach name in the timetable must match their first name. That's how the app shows them their own sessions.
12
How walk-ins appear in your roster
When a coach adds a new trial swimmer during a session using the Walk-in button, a roster entry is created automatically. They get the next available member number and appear in the Roster tab with a TRIAL badge, matching your R150 drop-in sessions.
Sets Planner & AI Coaching
13
Plan weekly training programmes
Open the Sets Planner from the Admin nav (or go directly to swimloading.com/sets/bluefin). You'll see a Mon–Sat calendar for the current week. Click any empty cell to assign a set. Click an assigned chip to view, edit, or remove it.
Three tabs in Sets Planner
Planner Weekly calendar: assign sets. A "Share week" button sends the full programme to WhatsApp.
Library All saved sets, filterable by focus (aerobic, endurance, speed, drills, kick). Edit or reuse any set.
New Set Build a set manually or generate one with AI. Choose session duration (your pool slot is a fixed 60 min), then describe the session and hit Generate.
14
Generate a training set with AI
In the New Set tab, set Session time to 60 mins to match your pool slot. The target distance updates automatically. Then describe the session in plain English and hit Generate set.
Example prompts that fit how you train
"Masters Squad, warm up/drill/scull, sprint 8x25, pull set, descending ladder, cool down: 60 minutes"
"Endurance-focused, Langebaan 6km prep, longer pull sets and steady pace work"
The AI writes a complete, formatted set with warm-up, main set, cool-down, and interval times. Edit it directly before saving. Once saved to the library it can be reused any week.
15
Share programmes to WhatsApp
Two share options, both open WhatsApp with the content pre-filled:
How to share
Single session Click a set chip in the Planner → tap the green WhatsApp button. Sends date, coach, and full set content.
Full week The Share week button sends every assigned session for the week, formatted with day, set name, and full content.
The message uses WhatsApp bold and italic formatting so it reads cleanly on the phone. No PDF, no printing, no group chat confusion.
16
AI Coaching Insights: training balance
In the Sets Planner, open the AI Insights tab and hit Analyse now. The AI reviews the last 4 weeks of training and returns recommendations.
What Insights shows
Training balance Flags if the squad is heavy on speed but missing endurance, or hasn't had a drills session in a few weeks.
Event-aware Add Langebaan Express or Robben Island programme dates to the Events tab so recommendations can account for how many weeks out your next event is.
One-click set generation Each recommendation has a "Generate this set" button that pre-fills the AI builder.
What your swimmers see
Swimmer view: Club tab in the SwimLoading app
  • Training schedule: next session at a glance
  • Announcements: pinned posts stay visible at the top
  • Their own attendance and set history
  • A log button to add their own training notes
