The science
Hydrogel technology — a different approach to fuelling

Traditional sports gels rely on sweeteners and flavours to make them palatable. Maurten's hydrogel matrix traps the carbohydrates so they pass through the stomach quickly, reaching the intestines where they absorb rapidly. The result: you can take on more carbohydrates per hour without the GI distress that derails long swims and races.

No sweeteners. No flavours. No additives.
Maurten's entire range contains only the essential carbohydrates, electrolytes, and water the body needs — nothing that gets in the way.
