Why JAKED
Research, ideas,
innovation.
JAKED is an Italian sportswear brand built around swim, open water and competition — technical, high-performance gear developed with the same research-driven approach that put Italian racing suits on the podium. Their own words: "from research come ideas, and from ideas comes innovation." It's a small brand with a serious performance ethos, exactly the kind of partner that fits SwimLoading.
Open Water Wetsuits
BLADE — JAKED's new open water wetsuit, built for the same conditions SwimLoading members log every day.
Competition Swimwear
Racing suits engineered for the pool — the category JAKED built its Italian racing pedigree on.
Goggles & Accessories
SPY EXTREME and the rest of JAKED's accessory range — "designed to support performance, in and out of the water."
JAKED Tribe
Their own community app — earn points, complete missions and climb a leaderboard. A gamified-loyalty streak worth watching alongside SwimLoading's own.
