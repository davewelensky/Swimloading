Why JAKED

Research, ideas,
innovation.

JAKED is an Italian sportswear brand built around swim, open water and competition: technical, high-performance gear developed with a research-driven approach. It has a serious performance ethos, exactly the kind of partner that fits SwimLoading.
Open Water Wetsuits
BLADE: JAKED's multi-thickness open water wetsuit, built for the same conditions SwimLoading members log every day.
Open Water & Competition Swimwear
J-KAI, JAKED's open water racing suit, was developed with open water marathon swimmer Domenico Acerenza. Pool racing suits are JAKED's original category.
Goggles & Accessories
SPY EXTREME and the rest of JAKED's accessory range: "designed to support performance, in and out of the water."
JAKED Tribe
Their own community app: earn points, complete missions and climb a leaderboard. A gamified-loyalty streak worth watching alongside SwimLoading's own.
JAKED athlete programme
#MoreThanSport
JAKED's #MoreThanSport project is about what goes beyond the race: the path, the determination and the steady growth that make an athlete stronger, in and out of sport. It backs athletes through three groups: ICON, established champions who turn experience into inspiration; SUPPORTER, personalities who share and amplify JAKED's values; and CREW, emerging talents the brand chooses to invest in. Open water specialist Domenico Acerenza, who J-KAI was developed with, is one of its ICON athletes.
See #MoreThanSport on jaked.com (in Italian)
