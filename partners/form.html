How to win
  • Swim on 12 different days from 1 December 2026 to 6 January 2027 (South African time) and log each swim on SwimLoading.
  • A swim you log yourself counts only if you log it the day you swam. A Strava swim counts on the day of the activity, and can be imported for up to 48 hours.
  • Every swim must be logged by midnight on 6 January.
  • 12 days gives you 1 entry. A swim on Christmas Day adds 1 and a swim on New Year's Day adds 1, so 3 entries at most. Bonus entries count once you have 12 days.
  • The prize is one pair of FORM Smart Swim 2 goggles, delivered to a South African address.
  • The organiser and their household cannot win. The winner is drawn at random on 7 January 2027. Fair play rules apply: only genuine swims count.
