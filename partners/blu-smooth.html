What makes Blu Smooth different
Built on Yamamoto neoprene

Yamamoto Corporation's closed-cell limestone neoprene is the benchmark for high-performance wetsuits — more buoyant, more flexible, and more durable than standard petroleum-based neoprene.

Yamamoto neoprene
Japanese-made limestone-based neoprene — higher buoyancy and better stretch retention than standard alternatives. Used by the world's top wetsuit brands.
