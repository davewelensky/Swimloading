## 1. Ahotu — referral partnership

**To:** contact@ahotu.com
**Subject:** Swimming vertical — a partnership, not a data request

Hi,

I run SwimLoading, an open water swimming platform. We list 329 upcoming
swims across 35 countries at https://www.swimloading.com/explore.

I've been looking at Ahotu, and the thing that stood out is that swimming
is your thinnest vertical — roughly 437 events out of 60,000. It is also
the only one we do. We have no interest in running, cycling or triathlon,
and no plans to.

That makes this a fit rather than a competition. What I'd propose:

- We send swimmers to Ahotu when an event you list has entries open. You
  keep the booking.
- You point swimmers at us for the things a booking marketplace does not
  cover: water temperature, conditions, and what a swim is actually like.
- Reciprocal links on the events we both carry.

We are not asking for a data feed and we are not asking you to do work.
If a referral partnership is interesting, I am happy to start with a
handful of events and see whether the traffic is worth either of our time.

Dave Welensky
SwimLoading
https://www.swimloading.com

## 3. OWAAF India — the one that actually needs an email

**Why email rather than crawl:** owaaf.in blocks automated fetches
outright (HTTP null through a polite, robots-respecting client). There is
no crawler fix for this — asking is the only route.

**To:** (address from owaaf.in — find the contact page by hand)
**Subject:** Listing India's open water swims and marathon routes

Hi,

I run SwimLoading, an open water swimming platform. We list 329 upcoming
swims across 35 countries, free to swimmers, at
https://www.swimloading.com/explore.

India is a real gap for us, and OWAAF looks like the reason it should not
be. Two separate things I would like to carry, if you are willing:

1. **Your events**, as they are scheduled — anything with a date and a
   place. Organisers can add these themselves at
   https://www.swimloading.com/list-your-swim, no account needed.

2. **Your WOWSA-certified routes** — Elephanta to the Gateway, Dharamtar,
   the Konkan fort-to-fort swims, Amini to Bangaram. These are not dated
   events, and we have a separate home for exactly that: a swim someone
   can arrange rather than enter. Very little of the world's marathon
   route network is catalogued anywhere a swimmer can actually find it.

Two things worth saying plainly. We would list these free, and we would
credit OWAAF as the source on every one. And we would rather take the
detail from you than scrape it — your site blocks automated access, which
I take as deliberate, so I am asking instead.

I swim open water myself — I did the first official night crossing of
Robben Island — so this is not a directory built by someone who has never
been cold.

Dave Welensky
SwimLoading
https://www.swimloading.com
