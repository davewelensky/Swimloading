Getting started
1
Open the coach register link

Open
2
Sign in the first time
You'll be asked to sign in with your email address. Enter it and you'll receive a magic link — tap it, and you're in. No password needed.
First-time setup — do this once
Enter your email address You'll get a sign-in link — check spam if it doesn't arrive in 30 seconds
Tap the link in your email Brings you straight back to the coach page, signed in
Add to your home screen On iPhone: tap Share → Add to Home Screen. Opens like an app, stays signed in.
After the first time
You'll stay signed in automatically. Just tap your home screen icon before each session — no login needed.
Marking the register
3
Tap your session
Your sessions appear on the home screen. Tap the one you're about to run — confirm your name — then tap Start marking attendance.
You'll see your session automatically
Sessions assigned to you Shown at the top — tap to open
Cover sessions Other squads' sessions — visible if you need to cover
Previous days Use the ‹ arrow to go back and mark yesterday's register if you missed it
4
Mark each swimmer
Every enrolled swimmer is listed. Tap the button next to their name to mark their status. Tap Save attendance when done.

Tip
You can save mid-session and come back — attendance is saved as you go. Tap Save when you're done and it's locked in.
Walk-ins & extras
5
Adding a walk-in swimmer
If a swimmer pitches up who isn't on the list — a trial swimmer, a catch-up from another squad, or a new face — tap Walk-in at the bottom of the attendance screen.
Walk-in options
Search existing roster If they're already in the system — tap their name to add them to this session
Add a new trial swimmer Enter their name — they get the next member number and a TRIAL badge. Britt will see them in Roster.
Common questions
?
I don't see my session
Your sessions are matched by your first name in the timetable. If your session isn't showing, check with Britt that your name is set correctly in the timetable — or tap Cover a session to access all sessions for today.
?
I forgot to mark yesterday
No problem — tap the ‹ arrow next to the date on the home screen to go back to any previous day. Open the session and mark as normal.
?
I got signed out
Just go back to swimloading.com/coach/aqua-sharks-atlantic, enter your email, and tap the link in your email. You'll be straight back in.
