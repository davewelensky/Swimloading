# SwimLoading — Partner & Sponsor Reference

> **Rule #1:** If a fact is not confirmed by the partner or their official site, mark it `[UNCONFIRMED]` and do not publish it. Never assume product details, prices, discount codes, prize values, or contact information. Ask before publishing.

> **Rule #2:** Prices change. Never hard-code prices on a partner page unless the partner has explicitly confirmed them and you are ready to maintain them. When in doubt, omit and link to their site.

> **Rule #3:** As SwimLoading expands (SA → UK → AUS → Switzerland), note which partners are SA-only and which are international-capable. Do not promote SA-only partners to international users without first confirming availability.

---

## Partner Page Checklist

Every partner must have all of the following before going live:

- [ ] Hero page at `/partners/<slug>.html`
- [ ] Route in `vercel.json`
- [ ] Card on `welcome.html` sponsors section
- [ ] All facts on the page confirmed by partner or their official site
- [ ] No fabricated product names, prices, or discount codes
- [ ] Contact name on file (even if not published)
- [ ] Geography confirmed (SA-only / ships to UK / AUS / international)

## International Expansion Notes

SwimLoading is expanding to UK, AUS, and Switzerland. Before surfacing any partner to international users:

1. **Confirm they ship to that country** — don't assume because they're a global brand
2. **Confirm the discount code / deal works in that country** — SA codes often don't apply on international sites
3. **Check if they have a local distributor** — Maurten via Art of Endurance is SA-specific; Maurten UK would need a separate arrangement
4. **Geography-gate partner promotions in the app** — SA partners should not appear in UK/AUS users' dashboards

## Process: Adding a New Partner

1. **Confirm the deal** — get a named contact, agree what each side offers
2. **Gather facts** — product names, correct URLs, discount code (test it), logo file
3. **Build hero page** — `/partners/<slug>.html` using the SwimLoading brand template
4. **Add route** to `vercel.json`
5. **Add card** to `welcome.html` sponsors section
6. **Review before pushing** — no unconfirmed facts, no fabricated details
7. **Record here** — fill in this document before the page goes live
