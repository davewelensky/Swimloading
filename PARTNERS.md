# SwimLoading — Partner & Sponsor Reference

> **Rule #1:** If a fact is not confirmed by the partner or their official site, mark it `[UNCONFIRMED]` and do not publish it. Never assume product details, prices, discount codes, prize values, or contact information. Ask before publishing.

> **Rule #2:** Prices change. Never hard-code prices on a partner page unless the partner has explicitly confirmed them and you are ready to maintain them. When in doubt, omit and link to their site.

> **Rule #3:** As SwimLoading expands (SA → UK → AUS → Switzerland), note which partners are SA-only and which are international-capable. Do not promote SA-only partners to international users without first confirming availability.

6. **Review before pushing** — no unconfirmed facts, no fabricated details
