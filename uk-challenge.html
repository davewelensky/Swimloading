Challenge Rules
How it works.
  • 10 points for every unique outdoor UK swim location you log — logging the same spot again doesn't earn more points, but it still counts toward your total swims.
  • Open to casual dippers, wild swimmers, sea/lake/river swimmers, lido swimmers, triathletes and Channel swimmers alike — a short dip qualifies the same as a long swim.
  • Outdoor UK locations only — sea, lake, river, dam, lagoon and outdoor tidal pools count; indoor swimming pools do not.
  • Don't see your spot? Propose it in the app — new locations are reviewed before they count toward the leaderboard.
  • Prize and winner-selection details will be announced before the challenge ends.
  • Conditions change rapidly — SwimLoading doesn't certify any location as safe. Always check local weather, tides, currents and access rules before swimming, and never swim alone where local guidance recommends otherwise.
