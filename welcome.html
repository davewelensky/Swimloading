Wherever you swim

Started at the tip of Africa.
Now the world's in.

Started by a handful of Cape Town ocean swimmers who were tired of guessing the temp. Word spread. KZN joined. The Garden Route. Gauteng (no ocean, but plenty of pools and dams), and they weren't taking no for an answer. Then the rest of the world. The water doesn't care where it is, and neither do we.

Crossing Intelligence

Training for a
big crossing?

The English Channel. Robben Island. North Channel. Every major open water crossing has its own temperature window, tide pattern, qualifying requirements and preparation arc. SwimLoading tracks all of it, and gives athletes a private preparation dashboard that goes beyond what Strava or Garmin can tell them.

Install SwimLoading on your phone

1
Open in Safari
You must use Safari, not Chrome or other browsers. Safari is the only browser on iPhone that supports installing web apps.
2
Tap the Share button
Tap the Share icon at the bottom of Safari (the square with an arrow pointing up).
3
Tap "Add to Home Screen"
Scroll down in the share menu until you see "Add to Home Screen" and tap it. You'll see the SwimLoading icon and name.
4
Tap "Add" in the top right
Confirm by tapping "Add". SwimLoading will now appear on your home screen just like a real app.
1
Open in Chrome
Open the app link in Google Chrome for the best experience. Samsung Internet also works.
2
Tap the menu (⋮)
Tap the three dots ⋮ in the top right corner of Chrome to open the menu.
3
Tap "Install app" or "Add to Home Screen"
Look for "Install app". If you don't see it, look for "Add to Home screen". Some phones may show a pop-up banner at the bottom automatically.
4
Tap "Install" to confirm
Confirm the install. SwimLoading will now appear on your home screen with its own icon, just like an app from the Play Store.
