  For Swim Clubs

The complete club
platform

Attendance register with history dots, AI-assisted training sets, qualifying time tracking, health monitoring, and a parent portal — all connected, all in one place. No spreadsheets. No printing. No WhatsApp chaos.

AI-Assisted Set Building

Describe what you need and get a structured draft to review and refine — not a finished product, but a solid starting point that saves the planning work. Build a reusable library by squad and focus.

Weekly Squad Planner

Assign sets to each squad across the week in a live calendar view. One screen, every squad, every day — with coach assignments per session.

WhatsApp in One Tap

Share a session or the full week's programme to WhatsApp in one tap. Coaches get it formatted and ready on their phone — no printing, no PDFs, no chaos.

NEW

Attendance History Dots

Six coloured dots next to every swimmer on the register — green for present, red for no contact, amber for away. One glance tells you who's consistent, who just came back, or who's on a streak. No spreadsheet required.

Health & Injury Tracking

Log injuries and illness per swimmer — shoulder, hip, knee, or general. Mild, moderate, or serious. Active issues appear as badges on the register and roster. Resolve when the swimmer is cleared.

Squad Tracker & QT Progress

Track PBs across all events. See who is within seconds of L1, L2, L3 or SANJ. The pipeline view shows every swimmer's qualifying progression at a glance.

AI Coaching Insights

Analyses training history and flags imbalances — too much speed, not enough endurance. Surfaces which swimmers are close to qualifying and what sets will get them there, with upcoming galas factored in.

Club Join Links

Generate a unique invite link. Swimmers tap it, create their account, and are automatically linked to your roster. Parents get their own link. No manual imports, no spreadsheet matching.

NEW

Cover Needed Alerts

When a coach can't make it, the system flags the session as "Needs Cover" and every other coach sees it live on their home screen. No more last-minute panics in the group chat.

Get Started Free
1
Open in Safari
You must use Safari — not Chrome or other browsers. Safari is the only browser on iPhone that supports installing web apps.
2
Tap the Share button
Tap the Share icon at the bottom of Safari (the square with an arrow pointing up).
3
Tap "Add to Home Screen"
Scroll down in the share menu until you see "Add to Home Screen" — tap it. You'll see the SwimLoading icon and name.
4
Tap "Add" in the top right
Confirm by tapping "Add". SwimLoading will now appear on your home screen just like a real app.
1
Open in Chrome
Open the app link in Google Chrome for the best experience. Samsung Internet also works.
2
Tap the menu (⋮)
Tap the three dots ⋮ in the top right corner of Chrome to open the menu.
3
Tap "Install app" or "Add to Home Screen"
Look for "Install app" — if you don't see it, look for "Add to Home screen". Some phones may show a pop-up banner at the bottom automatically.
4
Tap "Install" to confirm
Confirm the install. SwimLoading will now appear on your home screen with its own icon — just like an app from the Play Store.
