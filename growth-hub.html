Strategic Positioning
SwimLoading should not be positioned as only a temperature app. It is a global swimmer intelligence and community platform.
SwimLoading is
A swimmer intelligence platform
A community platform
A swim spot discovery tool
A conditions reporting system
A club and event growth platform
A prediction and planning tool for open water swimming
SwimLoading is not only
A temperature logger
A map
A WhatsApp replacement
