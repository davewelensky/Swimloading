We work with brands relevant to swimming, outdoor adventure, safety, equipment, recovery and wellbeing. Members get genuine benefits, partners get credible exposure, and SwimLoading builds a sustainable platform.

01

A member discount, product benefit or occasional prize in return for a defined listing and relevant recognition.

02
Featured campaign partner

Activation, prize and spotlight

An agreed activation, prize or product contribution plus a marketing fee where appropriate. Includes campaign placement, newsletter exposure and summary reporting.

03
Strategic partner

Multi-month programme

A negotiated, paid multi-month programme with agreed deliverables, content and measurement.

How we work
  • Partner exposure is agreed in advance; product contributions do not imply unlimited promotional placements.
  • We distinguish registered accounts, newsletter recipients, search impressions, clicks and actual community contributions.
  • We do not guarantee purchases, conversions or unique audience reach without supporting measurement.
  • We protect member trust: relevant brands, accurate product claims and useful community benefits come first.
