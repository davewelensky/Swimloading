The Audience
Not casual swimmers. Committed ones.

Every SwimLoading member is self-selected. They downloaded a niche PWA, set up an account, and come back regularly to log conditions. This is not passive scrolling.

Data-Driven Swimmers

They log water temperatures, track hazards, and read conditions before every swim. They make decisions based on information — including what gear and products to buy.

Event Seekers

145 upcoming swims planned through the app. These people show up. They coordinate, they train, and they travel for the right swim experience.

Spread Across Africa

Cape Town, Durban, Port Elizabeth, Kenton, Garden Route, Namibia. 10 regions and growing. Not a Cape Town bubble — a national community.

Performance Focused

Tracking streaks, competing on leaderboards, chasing monthly challenges. These are athletes who invest in their sport — in gear, nutrition, training, and experiences.
