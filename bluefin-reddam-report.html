The Programme
Building water confidence, one session at a time
8-week programme, one 30-minute session per week 7 of 8 sessions completed — the mid-term break, a storm, and college exams accounted for the gap
Coaching Sessions to date were run by Barbara Johnston-Read. Going forward, Scott Tait and Ursula Morris are coaching the Reddam College squad.
Kitted out at the introductory session Each student received a sports bag, costume, silicone cap, towel, soap and sunscreen
What sessions cover Safe pool entry/exit, water orientation, floating, kicking technique, breathing, arm-kick coordination, streamlining, and assisted/independent swims
Water Safety Levels
How to read this report
Water Safe
Jumps confidently into deep water and recovers unassisted into a back float, then swims safely to the side.
Water Safe — Shallow End
Comfortable and functional in shallow water, still developing confidence and ability for deeper water.
Not Yet Water Safe
Requires supervision and further lessons to build confidence, floating, breathing and independent movement.

A Highlight
Handstands and diving sticks
As confidence grew, lessons naturally progressed into more independent floating and basic stroke introduction. One highlight for many of the students was ending lessons with handstands and diving for diving sticks — moments that brought a lot of laughter, confidence and enjoyment into the pool.
