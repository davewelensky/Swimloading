SwimLoading CLDSA Awards 2026
Prepared for the CLDSA committee · AGM & Awards Evening · 10 September 2026 · Royal Cape Yacht Club

Better than a
lucky draw.

You asked whether SwimLoading could put up a lucky-draw prize for the awards evening. We'd rather your members earn it — so we built a live open-water quiz the whole room plays on their phones, with the winner decided on a big-screen leaderboard in front of everyone.

The five-minute version

1

Scan

A QR code goes up on the screen and table cards. Guests scan it with their phone camera — nothing to download.

2

Join

New guests create a free SwimLoading account in under a minute — name, email, password, done. Existing members just sign in.

3

Play

Six fun multiple-choice questions about cold-water swimming — the claw, afterdrop, the Benguela. 30 seconds each, big buttons, quick laughs.

4

Win

A live leaderboard runs on the projector as answers come in. When the quiz closes, first place lights up gold in front of the room.

Scoring is simple and fair: 10 points per correct answer, fastest total time breaks ties. No name out of a hat — the winner earned it, and everyone watched it happen.

The prize

A personalised SwimLoading Open Water Performance Assessment

The winner's swimming is measured with eo SwimBETTER handsets — the force-analysis technology used by Olympic swimmers — in partnership with Aqua Sharks Academy Atlantic.

Prize partners

And it doesn't end on the night: every guest who joins can keep logging swims on SwimLoading — one member who swims consistently between 1 August and 31 October wins a full eo SwimBETTER90 package.

What we need from the programme

Almost nothing — no stage time. The QR sits on the screen (or table cards) from arrivals at 18:00, guests join over drinks, and when Dave opens the quiz everyone plays from their seats — about five minutes on their phones.

The screen does the announcing: the leaderboard runs quietly in the background and first place lights up gold at the end. One line from the MC if you want it, none if you don't.

Dave runs everything from a laptop at his table — if the programme shifts on the night, the quiz moves with it.

What CLDSA gets

  • A high-energy interlude the whole room takes part in — phones up, laughing at the wrong answers, cheering the leaderboard.
  • Zero admin: no paper, no tickets, no counting. The screen announces the winner by first name and initial only — no private details shown.
  • A meaningful prize with a story — measured performance, not a raffle trinket.
  • Your members leave with a free SwimLoading account: live water temperatures, conditions and swim logging for the Cape's open water, built here.
