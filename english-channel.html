Water Temp by Month — Dover Strait
Swim season runs late June to early October. September and October are the warmest months due to thermal lag; the trade-off is shorter days and deteriorating weather. Qualifying swims (6h in ≤16°C) are typically done in May–June.

About the Crossing
Distance
33.8 km straight line; swimmers typically cover 35–40 km due to tidal sweep
Governing bodies
Channel Swimming Association (CSA) and Channel Swimming & Piloting Federation (CS&PF)
Season
Official CSA season: Apr 1 – Oct 31; practical swim window: late June to early October
Qualifying swim
6-hour swim in water ≤16°C (61°F)
World record
Andreas Waschburger, 6h 45m (Sep 2023) — England to France
First crossing
Matthew Webb, 21h 45m (1875)
Typical duration
12–16 hours average; slowest ~27 hours
Traffic
Busiest shipping lane in the world — ~600 tankers + 200 ferries daily
Completions
3,318 ratified solo swims since 1875 (LongSwims Database)
