admin guide

Roster management, daily attendance, squad capacity, gala results, QT tracking, and announcements — everything Britt needs to run Aqua Sharks without a spreadsheet.

Open Aqua Sharks Admin
Every day
1
Check the Overview — your weekly command centre
When you open the admin panel, the Overview tab is your starting point. The top strip shows this week's sessions across every day — click any day bar to switch the session list below it.
What Overview shows
Action items Fees overdue, trials expiring this week, swimmers not seen in 3 weeks — each alert links directly to the affected members
Week at a glance Mon–Sat bar chart: fill % per day, amber bar = coach gap. Click any day to see that day's sessions below
Session detail Tap any session row to see who's assigned — TRIAL and FEE DUE badges per swimmer — then mark attendance from the same panel
Squad health Last 30 days attendance rate per squad with trend (up or down vs the 30 days before)
Upcoming galas Next events with dates
KPI strip — always visible at the top
Today's fill Enrolled vs total capacity across all today's sessions
Coach cover Sessions with a coach assigned vs total — red if any uncovered
Attendance rate Last 30 days across the whole club
Open spots Available capacity across all sessions this week
Tip: Check Thursday and Friday on a Monday — if a session has no coach assigned (amber bar), you have time to fix it before the day arrives.
2
Mark attendance after each session
Go to the Attendance tab, select the session, and mark each swimmer. Seven status codes match your existing register exactly.
Status codes
Present — attended the session
NC (No Contact) — absent, no notice given
Notice — gave advance notice of absence
No Show — expected, did not arrive
Away — scheduled absence (holiday, travel)
DNP (Did Not Participate) — present but did not swim
Catch-up — attended a different squad's session
Setting up the squad
3
Import your roster from a spreadsheet
In the Roster tab, click Import CSV. Copy your member list (name + squad columns is enough), paste it in, map the columns, and import. Existing members are updated — new ones are added. Run it multiple times safely; it won't create duplicates.
What the importer handles
Squad name matching SENIORS, JUNIOR, GOLD etc. all resolve to the right squad automatically
Safe to re-run Re-import any time to fix squad assignments — existing records update, nothing duplicates
Flexible columns Map Name, Squad, Member No, DOB, Gender, Phone, Trial, Fee Paid
4
Set squad capacity in Settings
Go to Settings → Squads and click Edit on any squad. Set Max members to the squad's capacity. Once set, the Overview shows each squad colour-coded: green = open spots, amber = 1–2 left, red = FULL.
Current Aqua Sharks capacities
Senior Squad Max 23 members
Junior Squad Max 27 members
Gold Swim Fit Max 19 members
Silver Squad Max 17 members
Bronze Squad Max 13 members
5
Set up the weekly timetable
Go to Settings → Weekly Timetable. Add each recurring session — pick the squad, day, start and end time, session type, and assigned coach. These sessions power Today's Schedule on the Overview and are the sessions you mark attendance against.
Managing members
6
Trial swimmers and fee tracking
In the Roster tab, each row has inline toggles you can tap without opening the edit modal. The Overview alerts you when a trial is expiring within 7 days or a fee is overdue — clicking the alert takes you straight to the filtered list.
Inline toggles on every roster row
TRIAL × Tap to remove trial status immediately. Shows as + trial when the swimmer is a full member — tap to mark as trial.
UNPAID × Tap to mark fee as paid. Shows as + unpaid when fee is paid — tap to flag as owing.
Filter the roster by status
Trials filter Use the status dropdown to show only trial swimmers — useful at the end of each trial period
Fee overdue filter Shows only members where fee_paid is off and the due date has passed — click the Overview alert to jump here automatically
Tip: The Fees overdue and Trials ending stat cards on Overview link directly to the filtered Roster view — no need to manually change the filter.
7
Get swimmers onto the platform
Go to the Join Link tab and copy the swimmer link. Send it via WhatsApp. When they sign up and tap the link, they're matched to their roster entry — member number and gala history appear immediately. No manual linking needed.
What joining unlocks for a swimmer
Links to the roster Full gala history appears immediately
QT progress bars Shows distance to L2, L3, and SANJ
Time-history charts Every result plotted — up means faster
7b
Get parents onto the platform
Go to the Parents tab. Copy the parent join link and send it to parents — it's a different link from the swimmer one. Parents create their own account, search for their child by name, pick their relationship, and send you a request. You approve it in the same tab.
Managing parent access
Pending requests Tap Approve or Decline — parents can't see anything until approved
Approved parents Listed with their swimmer(s) and relationship. Revoke any time.
Manual invite Enter a parent's email + find their swimmer — useful if they won't self-register
Multiple parents per swimmer Mum, Dad, stepmum — each gets their own account and request. Approve each separately.
Multiple swimmers per parent Parent taps "Link another swimmer" after the first — siblings all linked to one account
Once approved, parents open the SwimLoading app, tap Club, and see a read-only view of their child's QT bars, gala results, time charts, upcoming events and squad announcements. They cannot edit anything or message you through the app.
After each gala
8
Enter gala results
Go to the Galas tab and select the event (or create it). For each swimmer who competed, enter stroke, distance, course (SC/LC), and time. SwimLoading automatically detects PBs and updates QT tier status.
What updates automatically
PB detection Compares to all previous results for that event
QT tier update L2, L3, or SANJ badge on the progress bar
Time-history chart New point added to each swimmer's graph
9
Log time trials between galas
In Squad Tracker, click any swimmer and use + Log time on any event. Adds a training point to their chart. Swimmers can also log their own times from the Club tab.
Qualifying times
10
SSA Age Group qualifying times — built in
SwimLoading uses the SSA Age Group 2025/2026 qualifying time tables. Once a swimmer's date of birth is on the roster, the app calculates their age group automatically.
The three tiers
L2 L3 SANJ
L2 is the entry-level qualifying time. L3 is the next step up. SANJ is the South African National Junior standard — the top tier. The progress bar shows the swimmer's current time as a dot on the scale. Green dot = PB.
Make sure every swimmer's date of birth is in Roster — without it, QT bars won't appear.
Keeping the squad informed
11
Post announcements
Go to Announcements. Write a title and message — gala deadlines, equipment lists, session cancellations. Pin important notices to keep them at the top. Appears immediately in every squad member's Club tab.
Good examples
Gala entry deadline Western Province Champs entries close Friday
Equipment list What to bring to Saturday's gala
Session change No Thursday session this week — public holiday
Managing coaches
12
Give coaches access to mark attendance
Coaches don't need a SwimLoading account first — it's created automatically when they sign in. You just add them in the Team tab → Coaching Staff → Add coach → enter their email address, then send them the coach link.
Adding a new coach — 2 steps
Add in Team tab Enter their email → they have access immediately
Send them the link WhatsApp or email: swimloading.com/coach/aqua-sharks-atlantic — they do the rest themselves
How the coach signs in (first time)
Coach opens the link and enters their email The page sends them a sign-in link by email — no password needed
They tap the link in their email It brings them straight back to the coach page, signed in and ready to go
Add to home screen from there Share → Add to Home Screen while on the coach page — not from the sign-in screen
Removing a coach
Team tab → Remove next to their name Instant lockout — they see "No coach access" if they try to open the page
Tip: The name in the timetable (Settings → Weekly Timetable → assigned coach) must match the coach's first name — that's how the app shows them their own sessions.
13
How walk-ins appear in your roster
When a coach adds a new trial swimmer during a session using the Walk-in button, a roster entry is created automatically. They get the next available member number and appear in the Roster tab with a TRIAL badge.
After a session with walk-ins
Check the roster Any new walk-ins added by coaches appear at the top with a TRIAL badge
Edit their details Open the Edit modal to assign the right squad, set a member number, and add trial start and end dates
Convert when ready Tap the TRIAL badge inline to remove trial status once they've joined officially
What your swimmers see
Swimmer view — Club tab in the SwimLoading app
  • Training schedule — next session at a glance
  • Announcements — pinned posts stay visible at the top
  • QT progress bars — distance to L2/L3/SANJ with their current time as a live dot
  • Time-history charts — all results plotted, PBs highlighted green — up means faster
  • Upcoming galas with dates and detail
  • A log button to add their own time trial results
Parent view — live
  • Parents log in with their own account — no sharing passwords with their swimmer
  • Club tab shows a purple "Following: [swimmer name]" card once approved
  • Full read-only view: QT progress bars, gala results, time charts, upcoming events, announcements
  • One parent account can follow multiple siblings — each linked separately
  • One swimmer can have multiple parents approved (Mum, Dad, guardian, etc.)
  • You approve every request from the Parents tab — nothing visible until you confirm
