admin guide

How to record gala results, track QT progress, post announcements, and give your squad and their parents a window into every swimmer's journey.

Open Aqua Sharks Admin
Getting started
1
Log into your admin panel
Go to swimloading.com/club-admin/aqua-sharks-atlantic and sign in with your SwimLoading account. You'll land on the Aqua Sharks dashboard — Members, Roster, Squad Tracker, Galas, and Announcements are all in the left sidebar.
swimloading.com/club-admin/aqua-sharks-atlantic
2
Get swimmers onto the platform
Go to the Join Link tab and copy the link. Send it to swimmers and parents via WhatsApp or email. When they sign up and tap the link, they're matched to their spot on the roster — their member number and gala history are waiting for them. No manual linking needed.
What joining does for a swimmer
Links their account to the squad roster Their full gala history appears immediately
Unlocks QT progress bars Shows how close they are to L2, L3, and SANJ
Shows their time-history charts Every result plotted — up means faster
Send this same link to parents. When a parent signs up using the join link, they can see their child's results after you link the accounts — useful for Carina to see Italia's progress without sharing Italia's login.
After each gala
3
Enter gala results
Go to the Galas tab and select the event (or create it if it doesn't exist yet). For each swimmer who competed, enter their stroke, distance, course (SC/LC), and time. SwimLoading automatically checks if it's a PB and shows the new QT status.
What gets updated automatically
PB detection Compares to all previous results for that event
QT tier update L2, L3, or SANJ badge on the progress bar
Time-history chart New point added to each swimmer's graph
4
Log time trials between galas
In Squad Tracker, click into any swimmer and use the + Log time button on any event. This adds a time trial point to their chart — useful to track training progress between competitions. Swimmers can also log their own times directly from their Club tab in the app.
Tracking squad progress
5
Use Squad Tracker for an overview
The Squad Tracker tab shows every swimmer — sessions attended, results recorded, and events swum. Click any swimmer card to see their full time history: every result across all events, plotted on a chart with QT lines and PBs highlighted.
What you see per swimmer
Sessions How many training sessions they've attended
Results Total recorded times (galas + time trials)
Events Number of gala events competed in
Qualifying times
6
SSA Age Group qualifying times — built in
SwimLoading uses the SSA Age Group 2025/2026 qualifying time tables. Once a swimmer's date of birth is on the roster, the app calculates their age group and shows progress bars for every event they've swum — showing how close they are to each tier.
The three tiers
L2 L3 SANJ
L2 is the entry-level age group qualifying time. L3 is the next step up. SANJ is the South African National Junior qualifying standard — the top tier. The progress bar shows the swimmer's current time as a dot on the scale, with each tier marked. Green dot = PB.
Make sure every swimmer's date of birth is entered in the Roster tab — without it, the QT bars won't appear. You can add or edit it anytime.
Keeping the squad informed
7
Post announcements
Go to the Announcements tab. Write a title and message — anything from gala entries closing, equipment lists, session changes, or training reminders. Pin important notices to keep them at the top. Announcements appear immediately in every squad member's Club tab in the app.
Good announcement examples
Gala entry deadline reminder Western Province Champs entries close Friday
Equipment list What to bring to Saturday's gala
Session changes No Thursday session this week — public holiday
What your swimmers and parents see
Swimmer view — Club tab in the SwimLoading app
  • Their training schedule — next session at a glance
  • Announcements from you — pinned posts stay visible at the top
  • QT progress bars — every event they've swum, showing distance to L2/L3/SANJ with their current time as a live dot on the scale
  • Time-history charts — one chart per event, all results plotted, PBs highlighted green, QT tier lines overlaid — up means faster
  • Upcoming galas with dates and detail
  • A log button to add their own time trial results between galas
Parent view (coming soon)
  • Log in with their own account — no sharing passwords with their swimmer
  • See their child's full results and QT progress
  • Receive the same announcements as the squad
Roster management
8
Keep the roster up to date
The Roster tab is your source of truth for the squad. Each swimmer has a member number, date of birth (needed for QTs), gender, category (squad / masters), and SASA number. Click any name to edit their details. You can also add new swimmers here before they've joined via the app.
Critical fields per swimmer
Date of birth Required for QT age group calculation
Gender Required for SSA qualifying times (M/F)
Member number Permanent identifier across all galas
9
Mark swimmers active or inactive
In the Members tab you'll see everyone who has joined via the link. Use the Active / Inactive toggle to manage swimmers who have left the squad or taken a break — they stay in the system (and their results are preserved) but don't appear in the active squad view. Tick Show inactive to see them anytime.
