Common questions
How do I access Pro after paying?
Email support@swimloading.com with your payment confirmation. We'll link Pro access to your SwimLoading account within 24 hours. In-app payments are coming — email is the current process.
How accurate are the predictions?
Validated within 90 seconds over a 5-hour Double RI crossing (Yolanda Carstens, March 2026). Accuracy depends on the quality of your pace input. Use the calibration tools for the best results. Add 5–10% buffer for safety planning.
What if I've never done open water?
The pool time converter works well as a starting point. Enter your fastest continuous 1km pool time and the tool corrects for push-offs, tumble turns, and open water conditions.
What about club access?
Club pricing gives your whole squad access under one subscription. See club plans →
What happens when my subscription expires?
You drop back to Free — live conditions on all routes remain available. Pro prediction features lock until renewal.
