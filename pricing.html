Common questions
How do I access Pro after paying?
Email support@swimloading.com with your payment confirmation. We'll link Pro access to your SwimLoading account within 24 hours. In-app payments are coming — email is the current process.
Is the Robben Island crossing really 4–5 swimmers a day?
Yes — Big Bay Events runs regular RI crossings throughout the year. The database has 920 verified finishes from 2014–2024 across skins and wetsuit categories. It's by far the most swum route in the collection.
How accurate are the predictions?
The model was validated within 90 seconds over a 5-hour Double RI crossing (Yolanda Carstens, March 2026). Accuracy depends heavily on the quality of your pace input. Use the calibration tools — a previous RI time or pool conversion — for the best results. Add 5–10% buffer for safety planning.
What if I've never done an open water swim?
The pool time converter works well as a starting point. Enter your fastest continuous 1km pool time and the tool corrects for push-offs, tumble turns, and open water conditions. The result won't be as precise as a swimmer with OW race history, but it's a solid planning estimate.
Does this work for relay teams?
The prediction models are designed for individual swimmers. For relays, use each individual swimmer's pace — relay dynamics (boat transfers, rest periods) are not yet modelled. Contact support if you have a relay team and we'll work with you directly.
What happens when my annual subscription expires?
You drop back to Free — live conditions on all 7 routes remain available. Pro prediction features lock until renewal. We'll send a reminder 2 weeks before expiry.
