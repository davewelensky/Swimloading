What you get
Inside the K8 club on SwimLoading
Log every swim
Tap Log after any session — ocean, dam, pool, or lagoon. Enter the water temperature and conditions. Your log goes to SwimLoading's conditions feed and counts toward K8 challenges automatically.
Group training sessions
Britt posts upcoming group sessions — location, time, distance, and session type. You'll see them in your Club tab. Tap Going / Maybe / Not going so Britt knows who's coming.
Challenges
K8 challenges run through SwimLoading — your logs count automatically toward any active challenge. Winners announced at the end of each challenge period.
Announcements
Important updates from Britt — session changes, safety notices, event info. Pinned posts stay at the top.
Health log
Log an injury or illness yourself — shoulder soreness, flu, anything affecting your training. Britt can see it and adjust your programme. Mark it resolved when you're back to full training.

Logging a swim — quick guide
A
After your session, tap Log
On the dashboard, tap the Log Conditions button. Pick the spot you swam at — search by name or use your GPS location. Enter the water temperature in °C. Add conditions (swell, wind, visibility) if you want, then tap Submit.
What to enter
Spot
Any open water spot — ocean, dam, pool, lagoon. Most Cape Town spots are already in the system.
Water temperature
In °C. A quick reading after you get out is fine.
Conditions (optional)
Swell, wind, visibility — helps other K8 members know what to expect at the same spot.
Every log counts. It goes to the SwimLoading public conditions feed and counts toward any active K8 challenge at the same time. You only need to log once.
