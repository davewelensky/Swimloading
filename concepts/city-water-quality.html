About these results

Enterococci are bacteria found in the gut of people and animals. They are counted in seawater samples as an indicator of faecal pollution, reported as colony-forming units (cfu) per 100 ml of sample.

Each result describes one sample on the date shown. It is not real-time water testing. The recent samples and the long-term classifications give the context for any single result.

Sampling, guideline values and classifications are the City of Cape Town's. The full methodology is published on Beach Alerts.

How the data would flow

The City's results would be shown where swimmers already plan their swim, attributed and linked back to Beach Alerts.

01
City publishes
Water-quality results, classifications and coastal alerts are published through Beach Alerts.
02
SwimLoading connects
City monitoring locations are matched to the appropriate SwimLoading swimming locations. Published results are then kept up to date automatically.
03
Swimmers see it
Shown on the spot page next to swimmer-logged water temperature, with City alerts alongside community hazard reports.
  • City results and classifications are presented without SwimLoading recalculating or reclassifying them.
  • Every result carries its sample date, and older results are labelled as not recent rather than shown as current.
  • A single sample is always shown with the recent results and the long-term classification alongside it.
  • Active City coastal alerts are displayed prominently alongside the water-quality information and linked back to Beach Alerts.
Proof of concept prepared by SwimLoading for discussion with City of Cape Town Coastal Management. It does not represent a partnership with, or an endorsement by, the City. The Big Bay water-quality results and classifications are City of Cape Town data from Beach Alerts, captured on 25 Sep 2026 and not updated since. The failure, alert and no-recent-sample states are simulated for discussion and are not City results or alerts. Water-quality results describe samples taken on the dates shown; they are not real-time water testing and do not describe the water on the day. The Big Bay frame is the live SwimLoading Big Bay page (water temperature logged by SwimLoading swimmers) with one section added: the City water-quality section, which is proposed and not on the live page today.
