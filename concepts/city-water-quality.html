How this would work

The City's results are shown where swimmers already plan their swim, attributed and linked back to Beach Alerts.

01
City publishes
Results and alerts are published to Beach Alerts, as they are today.
02
SwimLoading syncs
An automated sync reads the City's results for each matched beach and tidal pool.
03
Swimmers see it
Shown on the spot page next to swimmer-logged water temperature, wind and swell.
  • The City's result and classification are shown exactly as published. SwimLoading never recalculates or reclassifies them.
  • Every result carries its sample date, and older results are labelled as not recent rather than shown as current.
  • A single sample is always shown with the recent results and the long-term classification alongside it.
  • City alerts are shown in full, as issued, and every block links back to Beach Alerts.

Proof of concept prepared by SwimLoading for City of Cape Town Coastal Management. Water-quality results, classifications and alerts belong to the City of Cape Town and are shown for discussion only. Water temperature is logged by SwimLoading swimmers; wind and swell come from the Open-Meteo forecast model.
