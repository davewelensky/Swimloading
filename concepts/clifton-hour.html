The Format

Your hour.
Your swim.

A marked open-water course operates from Clifton 4th for one hour. Swimmers complete as much or as little as they want during that hour — there's no compulsory distance and no requirement to swim for the entire window.

No race pressure. Get in, swim your session, get out when you're done.

60 Minutes
One controlled swimming window.

The Model

We swim when the Atlantic gives us the window.

Clifton conditions can change rapidly. Instead of forcing every swim onto a rigid calendar, potential swimming windows are identified and conditions monitored — and the swim goes ahead when the ocean is ready.

The Course

The course follows the conditions.

There's no single permanent course. Approved course configurations are selected on the day, according to the conditions the water-safety team is seeing.
