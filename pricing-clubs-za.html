Most clubs run on a shared spreadsheet and a WhatsApp group. There's no attendance register, no session history, no health record, no way to see who's close to qualifying. That's the gap SwimLoading closes.
For Club Admins
Run your club from one dashboard
Roster & Squads
Full club roster with member numbers, squad assignments, roles (youth, senior, coach, social), and fee status. Filter, search, and edit any swimmer in seconds.
Club Join Links
Generate a unique invite link. Swimmers tap it, create their SwimLoading account, and are automatically linked to your club. Parents get their own role-based link. No manual imports.
Parent Portal
Parents link to their child's roster entry through a simple join link. Club admin approves the link. Parents see their child's squad and session info — nothing more.
Gala Management
Manage upcoming galas, track pre-entries per swimmer, and see fee status across the squad. One view for the whole competitive calendar.
For Coaches
Everything a coach needs on their phone
Mobile Attendance Register
Mark swimmers present, late, absent, or trial on your phone. Navigate back to previous sessions. Health alerts surface automatically next to swimmer names.
Health & Injury Tracking
Log injuries and illness per swimmer: shoulder, hip, knee, or general illness. Severity levels — mild, moderate, serious. Active issues appear as badges on the attendance register and admin roster. Resolve when the swimmer is cleared.
AI-Assisted Set Building
Describe what you need and get a structured starting point — not a finished product, but a solid draft that a coach reviews, adjusts, and owns. Build a reusable library organised by squad and focus.
WhatsApp in One Tap
Share a single session or the entire week's programme to WhatsApp with one tap. Coaches receive the full set formatted and ready — no PDFs, no printing, no copying from a whiteboard.
For Performance
See exactly who's close to qualifying
Squad Tracker & Qualifying Times
Track every swimmer's PBs across all events. See who is within seconds of L1, L2, L3 or SANJ qualifying times. The pipeline view shows your entire squad's progression at a glance.
AI Coaching Insights
Claude analyses your training history and flags imbalances — too much speed, not enough endurance. Surfaces which swimmers are close to qualifying and what sets will get them there, with upcoming galas factored in.
