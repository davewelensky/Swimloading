For Coaches
Everything a coach needs on their phone

The coach register is a mobile app. Open a session, mark swimmers in five seconds per swimmer, and WhatsApp the summary — all from the pool deck.

Attendance History at a Glance
Six coloured dots next to every swimmer's name — green for present, red for no contact, amber for away. See in one second who's consistent, who's just returned, or who's on a six-session streak. No spreadsheet required.
Cover Needed Alerts
When a coach marks a session as needing cover, every other coach sees a live "NEEDS COVER" badge on their home screen before the session starts. No more last-minute WhatsApp panics.
Mobile Attendance Register
Mark every swimmer present, NC, away, notice, or no-show in one tap. Navigate back to previous sessions to fill in missed days. Walk-in guests added mid-session. Health alerts surface automatically next to swimmer names.
Health & Injury Tracking
Log injuries per swimmer — shoulder, hip, knee, back, or general illness — with severity: mild, moderate, or serious. Active issues appear as bold badges on the attendance register and the admin roster. Resolve when the swimmer is cleared.

For Training
AI-assisted. Coach-approved.

Describe what you need — distance focus for a gala week, aerobic base for juniors, speed work for seniors — and get a structured, properly formatted set to review and own. Not a finished product; a solid first draft that cuts planning time in half.

AI-Assisted Set Building
Describe the session goal and the AI drafts a complete structured set — warmup, main set, cool-down — with intervals, rest periods, and coaching notes. You review, adjust, and publish it. The coach always owns the programme.
AI Coaching Insights
Every week, Claude analyses your training history and flags imbalances — too much speed, not enough endurance, approaching a gala without taper. Surfaces which swimmers are within seconds of qualifying and what sets will get them there.
Weekly Squad Planner
Assign sets to each squad across the full week in a live calendar. One screen shows every squad, every day, every coach assignment. Build a reusable library organised by squad and training focus.
WhatsApp in One Tap
Share a single session or the entire week's programme to WhatsApp in one tap. Coaches receive the full set formatted and ready — no PDFs, no printing, no copying from a whiteboard.
For Club Admins
Run your club from one dashboard

Everything you need to manage a growing club — roster, squads, galas, parent communication, and qualifying progress — without a spreadsheet in sight.

Roster & Squads
Full club roster with member numbers, squad assignments, roles (youth, senior, coach, social, non-competing), fee status, and trial tracking. Filter, search, and edit any swimmer in seconds. Export any time.
Club Join Links
Generate a unique link. Swimmers tap it, create their SwimLoading account, and are automatically linked to your club roster. Parents get their own role-based link. No manual imports, no data entry.
Parent Portal
Parents link to their child's roster entry through a simple join link. The admin approves the link. Parents see their child's squad and session info. Communication flows through the same platform — no extra app, no extra login.
Gala Management
Manage your competitive calendar: upcoming galas, pre-entry status per swimmer, and who has and hasn't paid. One view for every club admin and coach — no more gala spreadsheets.
For Performance
See exactly who's close to qualifying
Squad Tracker & Qualifying Times
Track every swimmer's personal bests across all events. See who is within seconds of L1, L2, L3, or SANJ qualifying times. The pipeline view shows your entire squad's progression at a glance — no more guessing who's ready to compete up.
Season Progress
Track improvement over the season against qualifying benchmarks. See trends by swimmer and by event. When a swimmer drops a PB, it updates immediately and their qualifying gaps recalculate across all events.
