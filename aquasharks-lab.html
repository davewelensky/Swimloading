0UNITS WASTED, EVERY STROKE

Where most of it goes

Straight down. Pressing the water towards the bottom of the pool lifts your chest a little and drops your hips and legs, so you end up dragging your own body along.

And the rest

Sideways. If your hand sweeps across the middle of your body on the way through, it pushes you off your line and you wobble instead of travelling.

None of this is strength

You are already making the force. It is pointing the wrong way. That is why this can change in a few sessions, while getting stronger takes years.

The best single swim measured in this lab got 62 out of 100 moving forward. More than double a typical club swimmer, from the same water and the same arms. That is the number worth chasing.

How a session runs

01

Warm up, then two short swims

Two 25m or two 50m efforts at about three quarters effort. Exactly the same every time, so the next session can be compared to this one.

02

Sensors sit in both hands

Nothing strapped to the body, nothing that changes how you swim. Most swimmers forget they are there after a length.

03

We find the one thing

There are always several faults. We pick the one that is costing the most, because trying to fix five at once fixes none of them.

04

Your page arrives within 24 hours

By WhatsApp or email. Yours to keep, and to show your coach. We want your coach to see it.

05

Come back and prove it stuck

The retest is against your own numbers, not anyone else's. That is the whole point.

We never coach against your coach. The report is yours and we want you to share it with whoever trains you. It usually backs up what they have been saying for months.
