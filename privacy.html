Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 January 2026

SwimLoading ("we," "our," "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information, in compliance with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) of South Africa.

1. Information We Collect

Account information

Email address, encrypted password, display name, full name, phone number, date of birth, home address (where provided).

Emergency contact information

Next of kin name and contact details, relationship, medical conditions and allergies (where provided).

Swimming data

Water temperature logs, location data (GPS coordinates and spot names), swim times and distances, event participation, self-reported swimming ability and cold-water experience.

Usage data

Device information, IP address, browser type, and usage patterns.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Safety: emergency contact notification, and (where you provide it) medical information for emergency responders
  • Service delivery: account management, event organisation, club and community features, performance tracking
  • Communication: service updates, safety alerts, event notifications, community messages

3. Legal Basis for Processing

Under POPIA, we process your information based on your consent, our legitimate interests in safety and service delivery, contractual necessity, and legal obligations.

4. What's Public — and What Isn't

SwimLoading is a community platform by design. Your display name, swimming activity, and the temperature/condition logs you submit are visible to the community — that visibility is the point, not a bug. Your emergency contact details and medical information are never shared with other users and remain private.

We do not sell your personal information. We share information with emergency services (e.g. police, ambulance, NSRI) and your designated emergency contact where genuinely necessary, and with service providers who help us run SwimLoading (database and email hosting).

5. Data Storage & International Transfers

Your data is stored on Supabase infrastructure. As SwimLoading serves swimmers in many countries, your data may be processed outside South Africa; we take reasonable steps to keep it appropriately protected wherever it's stored.

6. Data Retention

  • Active accounts: for the lifetime of your account
  • Deleted accounts: retained up to 12 months for legal/compliance purposes, then removed
  • Anonymised, aggregate data: may be retained indefinitely for statistics (e.g. "318 swims logged this month") — this never identifies you individually

7. Your Rights Under POPIA

You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your data; object to processing; receive your data in a portable format; and withdraw consent at any time. To exercise any of these, contact support@swimloading.com.

8. Children's Privacy

SwimLoading is not intended for children under 13. If you are under 18, you must have parental or guardian consent to use the service.

9. Cookies

We use essential cookies for authentication, session management, and preferences. You can disable cookies in your browser, but this may affect functionality.

10. Data Security

We use encrypted transmission (HTTPS), encrypted password storage, and access controls. No system is completely secure, and use of SwimLoading is at your own risk in that respect.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted here with an updated date. Continued use after a change constitutes acceptance of the update.

12. Contact

Data Protection contact: SwimLoading · support@swimloading.com

Complaints: if you believe your privacy rights have been violated, you may contact the Information Regulator (South Africa) at inforeg@justice.gov.za or +27 10 023 5200.
