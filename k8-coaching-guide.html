Getting started
1
Log into your admin panel
Go to swimloading.com/club-admin/k8-coaching and sign in with your SwimLoading account (britt@k8coaching.co.za). If you also manage Aquasharks, you'll see a club picker — select K8 Coaching to enter the K8 dashboard.
swimloading.com/club-admin/k8-coaching
2
Import your member roster
Go to the Roster tab and upload a CSV with your K8 members. This is the master list — members link their SwimLoading account to their roster entry via the join link. You can update the roster at any time without affecting existing links.
CSV format
Required columns first_name, last_name, email
Optional columns date_of_birth, gender, member_type
3
Share the join link with your members
Go to the Join Link tab. Copy the link and send it to your K8 members via WhatsApp. When they tap it and sign in (or create a SwimLoading account), they're automatically linked to their roster entry. Their swims and temp logs start counting toward K8 challenges immediately.
What happens when a member joins
SwimLoading account linked to K8 roster Matched by name from your imported list
They see the K8 Club tab in the app Their membership card, upcoming sessions, challenges
Their logs count toward K8 challenges Any temp log, any spot — counts for K8 internally
Training groups
4
The November Robben Island group is ready
A training group called "November Robben Island 2026" has already been created with a goal date of 15 November 2026. Go to the Groups tab to see it. You can edit the name, description, goal date, or close signups at any time.
November Robben Island 2026
Training group for the November 2026 Robben Island crossing attempt. Signup is open — members can join via the Roster tab once they're linked.
5
Add members to the group
In the Groups tab, click Manage members on the November RI group. Tick the members who are attempting the crossing. You can update this list as more people commit or drop out.
6
Post training sessions for the group
Click Post session on any group. Fill in the title, date, time, location, and distance. A WhatsApp-ready text is generated automatically — tap Share to WhatsApp to send it straight to your training group chat.
What to fill in
Title e.g. "6km open water training swim"
Location e.g. "C4, Cape Town" or "Langebaan Lagoon"
Distance In km — e.g. 6.0 for a 6km session
Session type Ocean / Dam / Pool / Race
7
See who's coming — RSVP tracking
Members tap Going / Maybe / Not going on each session in their app. Click View on any session in the admin to see the full RSVP list — useful for planning safety cover and transport.
8
Create more groups any time
Click New group to add more cohorts — for example a March Robben Island group, a Langebaan training group, or any other goal. Each group is independent — its own members, sessions, and challenges.
Challenges
9
Run monthly or goal-based challenges
Go to the Challenges tab to create an internal K8 challenge — for example "Log 8 ocean swims in July" or "Train 3x per week through October". Set a start and end date, define the scoring, and pick a winner type. Members see it in their Club tab.
Challenge options
Start and end date Any window — a week, a month, the whole season
Scoring actions Temp logs, training sessions, group attendance
Winner type Highest points or prize draw from qualifiers
Temp challenge
10
Track monthly temp logs automatically
The Temp Challenge tab automatically tracks every K8 member who logs water temperatures during the month — ocean, dam, pool, anywhere. Use the month picker to view any previous month. Useful for a simple monthly prize: most logs wins.
How points work
Every log Any swim, any spot, any time
10 pts
Unique spot bonus Per different spot logged per day
+10 pts
Health tracking
11
Log injuries and illness for your athletes
The Health tab lets you record when a member has an injury or illness — especially useful for the November RI cohort where you need to know if someone's shoulder is flaring up or they're sick before a long crossing. Members can also log their own health from the app.
What you can log
Injury Shoulder, hip, knee, other — with side and severity
Illness Cold, flu, or general illness — mild / moderate / significant
Resolve when cleared Mark as resolved when the member is back to full training
What your members see
Club tab in the SwimLoading app
  • Their K8 Coaching membership card — club name, member number
  • Upcoming group sessions they've been added to — with RSVP buttons
  • Active challenges and their current score
  • Their own health log — can add injuries or illness themselves
Important — how logging works
SwimLoading first, K8 second
Every temp log goes to SwimLoading It appears on the public conditions feed and counts for the SwimLoading June challenge
It also counts for K8 internally The Temp Challenge tab and any K8 challenges track it automatically
One SwimLoading account, multiple clubs A member can be in both K8 and Aquasharks — one login, separate club data
