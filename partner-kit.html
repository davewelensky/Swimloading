How we work
  • Partner exposure is agreed in advance; product contributions do not imply unlimited promotional placements.
  • We distinguish registered accounts, newsletter recipients, search impressions, clicks and actual community contributions.
  • We do not guarantee purchases, conversions or unique audience reach without supporting measurement.
  • We protect member trust: relevant brands, accurate product claims and useful community benefits come first.
