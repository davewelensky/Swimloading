Get started
Getting started: 3 steps
1
Tap the join link from Tracey
Tracey will share a Bluefin join link via WhatsApp. Tap it on your phone. If you already have a SwimLoading account you'll be linked to Bluefin automatically. No new account needed. If you're new, it takes about 60 seconds to sign up.
Already use SwimLoading? Tap the join link while signed in and you're added to Bluefin straight away. Your existing logs and settings are untouched.
2
Create a free account
Sign up with your email at swimloading.com/app. No App Store download. Once in, go to your profile and add it to your home screen so it works like an app.
When asked to pick your region, select "False Bay". Bluefin is your club (not a region), and the Bluefin club tab appears automatically once you've joined.
swimloading.com/app
3
Open the Club tab
Once you've joined via Tracey's link, a Club tab appears in the app. That's where Bluefin lives: your weekly timetable, training sets, and any announcements from Tracey.
What you get
Inside the Bluefin club on SwimLoading
Your weekly timetable
Pool Mon/Wed/Fri 7:45–8:45am and Tuesday open water 7:00–8:00am, always up to date in the app. No more digging for the right WhatsApp message.
This week's training set
The same set Tracey already writes for the squad, now in the app instead of a screenshot: warm-up through cool-down, ready before you get to the pool.
Announcements
Session changes, race entries, equipment reminders, from Tracey, pinned to the top of your Club tab.
Health log
Log an injury or niggle yourself: shoulder soreness, flu, anything affecting training. Tracey can see it and adjust your programme.
