Cameron Bellamy
New
South African open water swimmer based in San Francisco. One of the most decorated endurance athletes in the world — ocean crossings, polar rowing, and pursuit of records that redefine what's humanly possible in water.
World record — longest ocean channel swim · 151.7 km, Barbados to St Lucia in 56 hours 36 minutes (September 2019). Previous record: 124.4 km.
Drake Passage row · 665 mi across the world's most dangerous stretch of water with five others (December 2019). Documented by Discovery Channel in The Impossible Row.
Home water · Aquatic Park, San Francisco Bay — logging temps with the community.
